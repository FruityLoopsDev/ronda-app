# 🎬 PROMPTS DE GOOGLE FLOW (VEO 3.1) · 3 PUBLICACIONES DISRUPTIVAS

Este documento contiene los prompts listos para copiar y pegar en **Google Flow (Veo 3.1)** para animar en video las 3 publicaciones disruptivas de RONDA en Instagram.

---

## 🔒 REGLA DE CONTINUIDAD (Pegar al final de cada prompt)
```text
Shot on 35mm Kodak Vision3 500T, f/1.8 shallow depth of field, warm amber tungsten lighting, cinematic shadows, natural motion, no digital morphing, vertical 9:16.
```

---

## 1. VIDEO / REEL PARA EL POST 1: "EL INTERRUPTOR DEL SCROLL" (15 s)

### Concepto:
El video arranca mostrando un teléfono brillando en la oscuridad de una habitación y, en un movimiento continuo y cinematográfico, la cámara retrocede y vemos cómo se apaga la pantalla y se enciende una vela sobre una mesa de bar en Bogotá.

### Prompt para Google Flow (Veo 3.1):
```text
Cinematic vertical 9:16 shot starting with an extreme close-up of a smartphone screen glowing with cold blue light in a dark room. The camera smoothly pulls back in a continuous slow dolly motion. The blue screen light abruptly fades out as the smartphone is placed flat onto a dark walnut bar table. Simultaneously, a match strikes and lights a small amber beeswax candle beside it, bathing the wooden surface in warm golden tungsten light and rich shadows. Shot on 35mm Kodak Vision3 500T, f/1.8 shallow depth of field, warm amber tungsten lighting, cinematic shadows, natural motion, no digital morphing, vertical 9:16.
```

---

## 2. VIDEO / REEL PARA EL POST 2: "LA SILLA VACÍA" (10 s)

### Concepto:
Cámara lenta en traveling que entra por un gastrobar cálido de Bogotá y se detiene frente a una mesa para dos personas. Una copa humea sutilmente con hielo cristalino y la silla vacía en primer plano espera a su acompañante.

### Prompt para Google Flow (Veo 3.1):
```text
Slow, elegant cinematic push-in tracking shot through an intimate, atmospheric speakeasy cocktail lounge in Bogotá. The camera glides forward and settles on a corner table with an empty handcrafted wooden chair facing the viewer. On the dark aged oak tabletop, an artisanal cocktail with an orange twist and clear hand-carved ice sphere rests next to a gentle flickering candle. In the soft blurred background, amber-lit liquor bottles and silhouettes of people conversing. Inviting, mysterious, luxurious mood. Shot on 35mm Kodak Vision3 500T, f/1.8 shallow depth of field, warm amber tungsten lighting, cinematic shadows, natural motion, no digital morphing, vertical 9:16.
```

---

## 3. VIDEO / REEL PARA EL POST 3: "EL REPORTE DE PANTALLA" (8 s)

### Concepto:
Plano macro cenital (desde arriba) de una mano en un bar que desliza una última vez un teléfono mostrando gráficos de tiempo de pantalla, le da la vuelta bocabajo de forma decidida y toma una copa de cóctel para brindar.

### Prompt para Google Flow (Veo 3.1):
```text
Top-down macro shot over a dark wooden bar counter. A stylish hand holds a smartphone displaying analytics graphs, then with one smooth, decisive motion turns the phone completely face-down onto the table. The hand immediately reaches for an elegant crystal cocktail glass, lifting it slightly as if to make a toast. Warm amber candlelight glints off the crystal glass and dark wood grain. Shot on 35mm Kodak Vision3 500T, f/1.8 shallow depth of field, warm amber tungsten lighting, cinematic shadows, natural motion, no digital morphing, vertical 9:16.
```
